# Bias Audit Report

> **Disclaimer:** The metrics in this report are **synthetic demonstration values** created solely to illustrate a complete bias audit workflow. They are **not** empirical results obtained from executing Fairlearn or AIF360.

## Project
**Digital Transformation and Its Impact on the Quality and Efficiency of Higher Education: A Case Study of the Universidad Nacional Mayor de San Marcos**

## Objective
Evaluate algorithmic fairness using a standard benchmark workflow, demonstrate bias mitigation, and compare fairness metrics before and after mitigation.

## Dataset
- Adult Census Income (methodological reference)
- Protected attribute: `sex`
- Model: Logistic Regression
- Mitigation: Fairlearn ExponentiatedGradient

## Workflow

```text
Dataset
  ↓
Preprocessing
  ↓
Baseline Model
  ↓
Fairness Metrics
  ↓
Bias Mitigation
  ↓
Retraining
  ↓
Fairness Metrics
```

## Synthetic Results

| Metric | Before | After | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Accuracy | 0.851 | 0.842 | -0.009 |
| Precision | 0.844 | 0.839 | -0.005 |
| Recall | 0.812 | 0.825 | +0.013 |
| F1-score | 0.828 | 0.832 | +0.004 |
| ROC-AUC | 0.903 | 0.897 | -0.006 |
| Disparate Impact | 0.63 | 0.94 | +0.31 |
| Demographic Parity Difference | 0.24 | 0.06 | -0.18 |
| Demographic Parity Ratio | 0.67 | 0.96 | +0.29 |
| Equal Opportunity Difference | 0.18 | 0.04 | -0.14 |
| Equalized Odds Difference | 0.21 | 0.05 | -0.16 |

## Interpretation

The synthetic demonstration shows a substantial improvement in fairness after applying ExponentiatedGradient. Disparate Impact approaches the ideal value of 1.0 while parity and opportunity differences move closer to zero. A slight reduction in predictive accuracy is expected when fairness constraints are introduced.

## Limitations

- Results are simulated.
- No real fairness experiment was executed.
- Intended only to demonstrate repository structure and reporting format.

## Conclusion

This report provides a reproducible template for documenting bias assessment and mitigation. Actual values must be generated by executing the Fairlearn/AIF360 pipeline.
