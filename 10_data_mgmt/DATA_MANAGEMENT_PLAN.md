# Data Management Plan

## Project
Digital Transformation and Its Impact on the Quality and Efficiency of Higher Education: A Case Study of the Universidad Nacional Mayor de San Marcos.

## Scope
This plan describes the collection, documentation, storage, versioning, sharing, preservation, and disposal of research data.

## Data Types
- Public institutional indicators (UNMSM/SUNEDU)
- Synthetic dataset for ML pipeline
- Future anonymized research data (subject to ethics approval)

## Storage
GitHub (documentation), DVC (datasets), MLflow (experiments), encrypted local backup.

## Retention
Research records retained according to institutional policy; identifiable data will not be stored in the demonstration dataset.
