**Digital Transformation and Its Impact on the Quality and Efficiency of Higher Education: A Case Study of the Universidad Nacional Mayor de San Marcos**

# 1. Introduction

Digital transformation has become a strategic capability for higher education institutions. Beyond technology adoption, it encompasses governance, academic innovation, organizational change, data-driven decision making, and continuous institutional improvement. This protocol proposes a reproducible framework to evaluate its impact on educational quality and institutional efficiency.

---

# 2. Problem Statement

Public universities continue investing in digital transformation initiatives; however, reproducible methodologies capable of evaluating their institutional effects remain limited. Existing studies frequently emphasize technology implementation instead of measurable institutional outcomes.

---

# 3. Research Questions

## General Question

How does digital transformation influence the quality and efficiency of higher education at the Universidad Nacional Mayor de San Marcos?

## Specific Questions

1. Which dimensions of digital transformation significantly influence educational quality?
2. How does digital governance affect institutional efficiency?
3. Which indicators best explain institutional digital maturity?
4. How can reproducible Machine Learning support institutional evaluation and decision making?

---

# 4. Research Objectives

## General Objective

To evaluate the influence of digital transformation on educational quality and institutional efficiency through a reproducible mixed-methods framework.

## Specific Objectives

- Assess institutional digital transformation maturity.
- Analyze educational quality indicators.
- Evaluate operational efficiency.
- Develop a reproducible machine learning workflow.
- Produce evidence-based recommendations.
