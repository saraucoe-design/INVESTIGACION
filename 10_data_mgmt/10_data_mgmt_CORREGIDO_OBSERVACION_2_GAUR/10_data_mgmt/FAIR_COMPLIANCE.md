# FAIR Compliance

## Findable
Versioned repository, metadata, consistent filenames, and a data dictionary mapping variables to research constructs and dimensions.

## Accessible
Controlled access; documentation in Markdown. Real institutional data require authorization and ethics approval.

## Interoperable
Open formats: CSV, JSON, YAML, Markdown. Variable names, coding rules, construct mappings, and provenance will be documented.

## Reusable
MIT (code), CC BY 4.0 (documentation), documented provenance and data dictionary. Empirical reuse requires documentation of the validated/selected instrument, scoring procedure, construct-to-variable mapping, and applicable authorization constraints.
