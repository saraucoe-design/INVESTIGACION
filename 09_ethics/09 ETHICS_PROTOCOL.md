# Ethics Protocol

**Version:** 1.0  
**Last Updated:** August 2026  

## Project

**Digital Transformation and Its Impact on the Quality and Efficiency of Higher Education: A Case Study of the Universidad Nacional Mayor de San Marcos**

---

# 1. Purpose

This Ethics Protocol defines the ethical principles, governance framework, participant protections, and data management procedures for the doctoral research project. It aligns with international research ethics, Peruvian legislation, and institutional requirements to ensure responsible, transparent, and reproducible research.

---

# 2. Ethical Framework

This protocol is based on:

- Belmont Report (1979)
- Mitchell et al. (2019) — *Model Cards for Model Reporting*
- Gebru et al. (2021) — *Datasheets for Datasets*
- CONCYTEC National Code of Scientific Integrity
- Peruvian Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 29733)
- Applicable UNMSM regulations governing research ethics

---

# 3. Research Summary

## Objective

To evaluate how digital transformation influences educational quality and institutional efficiency at the Universidad Nacional Mayor de San Marcos using a reproducible Machine Learning pipeline.

## Methodology

- Mixed-methods approach
- Quasi-experimental design
- Machine Learning classification
- Git + DVC + MLflow reproducible workflow

---

# 4. Belmont Principles

## Respect for Persons

Participation is voluntary.

Participants will:

- receive complete study information;
- provide informed consent;
- be free to withdraw at any time;
- experience no academic or administrative consequences for non-participation.

Personally identifiable information will not be included in analytical datasets.

## Beneficence

Expected benefits include:

- improved institutional digital transformation;
- better evidence for decision-making;
- enhanced educational quality;
- increased administrative efficiency.

Potential risks:

- disclosure of institutional information;
- algorithmic bias;
- misuse of predictive outputs.

Risk mitigation measures are described throughout this protocol.

## Justice

Participant selection will be equitable and transparent.

No discrimination will occur based on gender, age, ethnicity, disability, socioeconomic status, or academic role.

# 6. Data Sources

## Public Institutional Data

- SUNEDU institutional indicators
- UNMSM public reports
- Strategic planning documents
- Institutional statistics

## Synthetic Dataset

A synthetic dataset of 600 observations supports demonstration of the reproducible Machine Learning pipeline.

The synthetic records do not represent real individuals.

## Future Empirical Data

Any future collection of real institutional data will require prior ethical approval and institutional authorization.

# 7. Data Protection

The project complies with Peru's Personal Data Protection Law.

Safeguards include:

- anonymization;
- restricted access;
- encrypted storage;
- secure backups;
- controlled retention;
- secure disposal after project completion.

- duration;

Infrastructure:

# 10. Artificial Intelligence Governance

The Machine Learning model supports decision-making and must never replace human judgment.

Responsible AI principles include:

- transparency;
- explainability;
- fairness;
- accountability;
- human oversight.

---

# 11. Risk Assessment

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|--------|------------|
| Data leakage | Low | High | Encryption and restricted access |
| Re-identification | Low | High | Anonymization |
| Algorithmic bias | Medium | Medium | Periodic validation |
| Misinterpretation | Medium | Medium | Human review |
| Unauthorized access | Low | High | Authentication and audit logs |

Overall ethical risk: **Low–Moderate**.
