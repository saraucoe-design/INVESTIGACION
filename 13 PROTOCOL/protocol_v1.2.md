# Project Title

**Digital Transformation and Its Impact on the Quality and Efficiency of Higher Education: A Case Study of the Universidad Nacional Mayor de San Marcos**

## 1. Background

Digital transformation has evolved from isolated technological adoption to an institutional strategy affecting governance, teaching, research, and administrative services. This protocol emphasizes evidence-based evaluation using reproducible research practices.

## 2. Refined Problem Statement

Previous versions identified the lack of empirical evidence regarding digital transformation. This revision specifies the methodological gap: public universities lack integrated and reproducible evaluation frameworks combining institutional indicators, qualitative evidence, and machine learning.

## 3. Refined Research Questions

### General
How does digital transformation influence educational quality and institutional efficiency at UNMSM?

### Specific
1. Which dimensions of digital transformation have the greatest institutional impact?
2. How are quality indicators associated with digital transformation maturity?
3. How does institutional digital governance affect operational efficiency?
4. How can reproducible machine learning support institutional decision making?

## 4. Objectives

### General
Evaluate the influence of digital transformation on educational quality and institutional efficiency through a reproducible mixed-methods framework.

### Specific
- Analyze institutional digital maturity.
- Measure quality and efficiency indicators.
- Develop and document a reproducible ML pipeline.
- Produce evidence-based recommendations.
