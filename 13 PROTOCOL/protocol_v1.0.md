**Version:** 1.0

## Project Title

**Digital Transformation and Its Impact on the Quality and Efficiency of Higher Education: A Case Study of the Universidad Nacional Mayor de San Marcos**

---

# Abstract

This protocol presents the first complete draft of a doctoral research project investigating how digital transformation influences the quality and efficiency of higher education at the Universidad Nacional Mayor de San Marcos (UNMSM). A pragmatic mixed-methods approach integrates institutional indicators, qualitative evidence, and a reproducible machine learning workflow based on Git, DVC, and MLflow.

# Keywords

Digital Transformation; Higher Education; Educational Quality; Institutional Efficiency; Machine Learning; Reproducible Research; UNMSM

---

# 1. Introduction

Digital transformation has become a strategic priority for higher education institutions. Universities increasingly rely on digital technologies to improve academic quality, administrative efficiency, governance, and evidence-based decision making. Nevertheless, there is limited empirical evidence explaining how these initiatives affect institutional performance in public universities.

# 2. Problem Statement

Despite sustained investments in digital infrastructure and services, universities often lack reproducible evaluation frameworks capable of measuring the institutional effects of digital transformation.

# 3. Research Questions

## General Question

How does digital transformation influence the quality and efficiency of higher education at UNMSM?

## Specific Questions

1. How does digital infrastructure affect educational quality?
2. How does digital governance improve institutional efficiency?
3. Which digital transformation dimensions have the greatest institutional impact?
4. How can reproducible machine learning support institutional evaluation?

# 4. Objectives

## General Objective

Analyze the influence of digital transformation on educational quality and institutional efficiency at UNMSM.

## Specific Objectives

- Evaluate digital transformation dimensions.
- Measure educational quality indicators.
- Assess institutional efficiency.
- Develop a reproducible ML framework.
