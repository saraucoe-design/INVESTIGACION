# Research Protocol v0.1

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Initial Draft  
**Project:** Digital Transformation and Its Impact on the Quality and Efficiency of Higher Education: A Case Study of the Universidad Nacional Mayor de San Marcos

## 1. Background
Digital transformation is reshaping higher education through digital governance, learning technologies, institutional analytics, and process automation. Public universities require evidence-based approaches to evaluate whether these initiatives improve educational quality and operational efficiency.

## 2. Problem Statement
Although universities invest in digital transformation, limited empirical evidence explains its institutional impact using reproducible research practices.

## 3. Research Question
How does digital transformation influence the quality and efficiency of higher education at the Universidad Nacional Mayor de San Marcos?

### Specific Questions
1. How does digital infrastructure influence educational quality?
2. How does digital governance affect institutional efficiency?
3. Which dimensions of digital transformation are most influential?
4. How can reproducible Machine Learning support institutional evaluation?

## 4. Objectives

### General
Analyze the influence of digital transformation on educational quality and institutional efficiency at UNMSM.

### Specific
- Evaluate digital transformation dimensions.
- Measure institutional quality indicators.
- Analyze operational efficiency.
- Build a reproducible ML workflow.

## 5. Research Paradigm
Pragmatism with a mixed-methods approach integrating quantitative institutional indicators and qualitative contextual analysis.

## 6. Methodology
- Mixed Methods
- Quasi-experimental design
- Case study
- Statistical analysis and Machine Learning

## 7. Variables
**Independent:** Digital Transformation

**Dependent:** Educational Quality and Institutional Efficiency

## 8. Ethical Considerations
The study follows the Belmont Report, CONCYTEC integrity principles, Peru Law No. 29733, and applicable UNMSM ethics requirements.

## 9. Expected Contributions
- Evidence for institutional decision-making.
- Reproducible ML pipeline.
- FAIR data management.
- Responsible AI practices.
